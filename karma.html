karma and panch tatvas

This is direction you give to your karma. It follows light. Solidity is any substance added to karma. For example- if you do a job, your substance is your job. Air - this is the substance you consume. In order to thrive and survive, you depend on air. It symbolizes dependency. Liquidity symbolises flow. Autopilot mode of action is a component of liquidity.
